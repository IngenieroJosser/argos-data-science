# M8 Start — 2026-09-23

**Decision:** M8 is authorized as the final ARGOS technical-development milestone while M7 continues prospectively on frozen ARGOS V1.

## Frozen inheritance

- M6 remains closed and immutable as the operational V1 baseline.
- M7 protocol/model/calibrator/config/scoring-contract hashes must remain unchanged.
- TEST 2025 and fresh OOT 2026 are consumed and cannot be used for M8 feature/model selection.

## M8 scope — `m8-advanced-intelligence-v2`

1. Semantic NLP on point-in-time-safe contract text.
2. Temporal supplier–entity and co-bidding graph intelligence.
3. Contextual anomaly detection fit on TRAIN only.
4. Combined PIT audit.
5. Fair temporal ablation versus the tabular baseline using a shared random seed.
6. Survival outcomes with explicit administrative censoring at 180 days.
7. Kaplan–Meier + penalized Cox PH fit on TRAIN and evaluated on VALIDATION.
8. Explicit promote/no-promote decision for advanced feature families without claiming final V2 generalization.

## Closure rule

M8 closes on evidence, not on a requirement that advanced features outperform the baseline. A no-promotion result is valid if NLP, graph, anomaly, survival, lineage, PIT checks and governance controls are complete.

M7 remains an independent longitudinal experiment and is not artificially closed by M8.
