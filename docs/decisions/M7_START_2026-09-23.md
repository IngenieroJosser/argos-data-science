# M7 Start Decision — 2026-09-23

**Decision:** M7 STARTED — `Prospective Validation & Operational Evaluation`.

## Scope

The seventh V1 milestone is reserved for genuinely prospective evaluation. M5 already absorbed advanced robustness work and M6 already closed productionization/MLOps infrastructure; M7 therefore does not duplicate either milestone.

## Scientific boundary

Primary M7 evidence must come from contracts whose `t0` is strictly after the protocol freeze. TEST 2025 and the M5 OOT 2026 cohort remain consumed and may be cited only as historical benchmarks.

With first eligible `t0` on 2026-09-24 and the frozen 180-day horizon, the first contractual maturity date is 2027-03-23. A reporting-lag buffer and the requirement for multiple matured cohorts push final closure later.

## Governance

ARGOS remains batch-first, shadow and human-in-the-loop. M7 does not authorize automatic production promotion, sanctions, eligibility decisions, corruption/fraud findings or legal conclusions.
